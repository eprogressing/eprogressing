## Naming and source notes

- Competition names follow the NBJL personal page where available, including the parent competition and the OS Function Challenge Track. CUMCM uses “China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling”; the Tianjin division is stated explicitly.
- The 2024 data-analysis competition is supported by the author-provided presentation. Its English name is a translation of the Chinese title; it is not presented as a verified organizer-issued English title.
- The TRAE workshop runner-up result is supported by both the NBJL page and slide 5 of the presentation. It is retained at the author’s explicit request, despite its C-category classification in the presentation.
- The compiler implementation contest’s wildcard advancement is a progression result, not a prize, and is excluded from this award list.
- Other university-level contests, scholarships, and service honors are outside this competition list.
- Education on the NBJL personal page states that the PhD is expected to begin in 2027; the profile therefore describes it as future study.
